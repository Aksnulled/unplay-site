FAQ

Questions studios ask us

What is a playable ad?

A short interactive version of your game that runs inside the ad. The player tries the core gesture for 15 to 30 seconds, then sees the install button.

How fast can you deliver?

A first playable build in 3 days for most casual and puzzle games. Final exports and one round of changes follow right after.

Which formats do you deliver?

One HTML5 file under 2 MB, adapted to each network's rules (MRAID, sizes, store redirects).

Do you use our art?

Yes. Send your assets or your store page and we match your game's look. The demos on this page use our own placeholder art.

Can you make several variants?

Yes. Different hooks, levels and end cards from the same build, so you can test what converts best.
